# Axisymmetric photon collapse

The matter is a finite-width distribution of collisionless, massless particles on a spacetime with an axial Killing vector.

## Language

**Photon intensity** is the energy carried per unit proper volume and local solid angle, integrated over photon frequency in the Eulerian frame.

**Prograde radiation** has positive axial Killing momentum. **Retrograde radiation** has negative axial Killing momentum. Each photon retains its sign through a radial turning point.

**Exterior photon angular momentum** is the integral of the photon axial Killing current outside the accepted horizon on a spatial slice. Before formation this integral covers the entire slice.

**Horizon extremality gap** is one minus eight pi times the absolute quasi-local axial spin divided by the horizon area, in geometric units.

## Example

“What happens to the angular momentum when a prograde beam turns around?” Its outward flux changes the exterior budget. Each photon still carries positive axial Killing momentum.
