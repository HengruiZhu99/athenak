# Figure 3 reproduction assessment

The AthenaK curves were overlaid against the authenticated Bamps and Prague
reference curves without time shifts, amplitude shifts, rescaling, or manual
alignment. The primary plot is
`analysis/history/figures/figure3_three_resolution.pdf`; the required minimum
and rebound zoom is
`analysis/history/figures/figure3_peak_minimum_rebound_zoom.pdf`.

## Verdict

The reproduction failed before the scientifically relevant Figure-3 region.
The common AthenaK interval ends at central proper time approximately 8.827.
The published first peak is near tau 10.31 and the deep minimum near tau 12.62.
Consequently none of the three AthenaK resolutions reaches the published first
peak, the descent to the minimum, the minimum, or rebound.

The central observable therefore cannot be declared convergent in the Figure-3
region. The early traces agree far better than the noncentral constraint mode,
but that is only an early-time consistency observation.

No horizon or critical-collapse conclusion follows from this campaign.
