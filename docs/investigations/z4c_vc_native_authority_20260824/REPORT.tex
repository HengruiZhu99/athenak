\begin{abstract}
We generated a fresh N256 vertex-centered Cartoon AMR authority after repair
of derefinement-slot corruption, verified deterministic record/replay, and
replayed the authority at N128 and N512.  The repaired scheme converges cleanly
through central proper time $\tau\simeq4$.  A later N256 extension develops
large constraints on a fixed 44-leaf hierarchy before native refinement
activates, then undergoes a refinement cascade to logical level 23 and stalls
near $t=11.192887945M$.  The result qualifies the early window but is neither a
full Figure-3 reproduction nor a convergence result for late collapse.
\end{abstract}

\section{Authority and deterministic replay}

The reviewed numerical baseline is commit
\texttt{6dd20656a305f2543bbbd7001550c6ac67019180}.  A diagnostics-only repair at
\texttt{d63519328214a6315a9cc1f7d5e4a1aa4bca21b0} replaces unordered CUDA
atomics in regional history sums with a Kokkos reduction.  It changes no
evolution variable, AMR decision, transfer operator, equation, gauge, RK, KO,
or CFL choice.  In the post-repair aggregate host run, 140/141 enabled tests
passed; the sole AMR-history timeout passed in isolation in 177.96 seconds.
The focused one-A100 suite passed 20/20 tests.

N256 record and replay have identical accepted events, times, checksums,
history bytes, auxiliary diagnostics, and numerical payloads for all 33 binary
outputs and six restarts.  Complete binary/restart containers differ in their
authenticated parameter headers because one records mode \texttt{record} and
the other \texttt{replay}; numerical identity follows the repository contract
and hashes bytes after \texttt{<par\_end>}.

The repaired and historical authorities agree exactly through event 3.  The
historical tree immediately re-refines at $t=0.30305969M$ to 74 leaves.  The
repaired tree instead stays at 44 leaves through $t=6.5M$ and next refines only
at $t=10.27866870M$ (Figure~\ref{fig:authority}).

\section{Native-AMR failure before $\tau\simeq7$}

The later N256 segment first exceeds $C=0.01$ at $t=7.2894893M$
($\tau=4.45109$) and $C=1$ at $t=9.2042653M$ ($\tau=5.61265$), while the
hierarchy remains fixed at 44 leaves and physical level 1.  The first late
refinement occurs only at $t=10.2786687M$ ($\tau\simeq6.28$), when the nearest
history sample already has $C\simeq20.9$.

The hierarchy then reaches logical level 7 at $t=11.1038229M$.  Event 22 at
$t=11.19248494M$ reaches logical level 14 and begins an effectively
coordinate-time-stagnant cascade.  At cancellation, event 159 has 1,367
leaves, logical level 23, physical refinement level 20, and
$\Delta t=1.07\times10^{-8}M$.  The global $C$ diagnostic is
$1.40\times10^{14}$ and the domain maximum absolute Kretschmann scalar is
$1.60\times10^{25}$, while the sampled axis-center value is only
$7.40\times10^{-3}$.  The runaway is therefore noncentral in this diagnostic.

The failed authority contains 160 accepted hierarchy states and 159 topology
transactions: 144 grow, 13 shrink, and two preserve the leaf inventory.  It
records 1,521 created leaves, 186 deleted leaves, 888 balance-induced leaves,
and 21 sign changes between successive leaf-count increments.  The inventory
peaks at 1,373 leaves and terminates at 1,367.  Refinement and derefinement
requests coexist in 146 event records; these are per-MeshBlock diagnostic
flags, not accepted-coarsening counts.

\section{Interpretation and qualification boundary}

Observation: substantial constraint growth begins on the fixed parent
hierarchy before the refinement cascade.  Inference: the current chi-only
sensor activates late relative to the shortest or error-bearing scale, or a
bulk numerical mode grows before refinement exposes it.  Hypothesis: repeated
high-order transfer and interface coupling then accelerates the existing
error.  This campaign does not distinguish those mechanisms and does not
establish a source-level bug.

Job 57525753 was cancelled at the fail gate.  No N128/N512 replay was launched
for the failed authority, and no $\tau\simeq10.5$ or full published-interval
calculation was attempted.  Figure~\ref{fig:fig3} is only a direct, unshifted
overlay over the qualified partial interval.
