Observed failure mechanism
--------------------------
Raw ordinary-gauge frame components are not puncture regular.  From n=16 to
48, |Hhat_A|_inf grew 7.48325 -> 125.126 and |theta_A|_inf grew 19.3337 ->
1786.14.  The raw n=24 and n=32 exact stationary runs aborted at approximately
t=0.828 and t=0.629 respectively.  Failure moved earlier under refinement.

Equation-preserving correction
------------------------------
For static references the stored variables are

  delta_Hhat_A = Hhat_A - Href_A
  delta_theta_A = theta_A - theta_ref_A.

Href, theta_ref, and partial_mu Href are reconstructed analytically from the
reference two-jet.  The physical driver, GH source, gauge constraint, and
boundary data reconstruct the same raw Hhat and theta.  Spatial differencing
and KO act only on regular differences.  Time-dependent reference subtraction
fails closed until an analytic partial_t theta_ref is implemented.
