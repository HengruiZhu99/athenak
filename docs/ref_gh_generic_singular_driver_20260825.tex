\begin{abstract}
This report records the first implementation checkpoint for a generic
puncture-adapted reference-frame generalized-harmonic system.  A
reference-independent first-order-state puncture-exponent estimator and a
Gaussian-localized analytic singular reference were implemented and exercised
locally.  The analytic estimator and the prescribed reference-jet scan pass,
but the required direct-finite-difference comparison fails for a scale
invariance reason on the prescribed shell.  Therefore closed-loop control is
still \status{stopped at the initial estimator hard gate}. The subsequent
standard $\gamma_2$, hyperbolic gauge-driver, exact stationary-trumpet, and
regular perturbed-trumpet local gates have advanced. No closed-loop control,
wormhole evolution, SMR, or PVC qualification claim is made.  The current
mixed-time gauge-reference and temporal evidence uses a puncture-clear
finite-difference support mask.
\end{abstract}

\section{Primary equations and convention map}

Ordinary GH uses
\begin{equation}
 C^a=\widehat H^a+g^{bc}\Gamma^a{}_{bc},
\end{equation}
while the background-covariant form is
\begin{equation}
 C^a=H_{\rm bg}^a+g^{bc}
       \left(\Gamma^a{}_{bc}-\bar\Gamma^a{}_{bc}\right).
\end{equation}
Equality of the physical constraint gives the sign relation
\begin{equation}
 H_{\rm bg}^a=\widehat H^a+g^{bc}\bar\Gamma^a{}_{bc}.
\end{equation}
Production reconstructs this relation from evolved ordinary
$\widehat H_A$ and the current reference geometry.

The runtime $\gamma_2$ additions and Einstein characteristic map implement
Eqs.~(35)--(37) and (32)--(34) of Ref.~\cite{lindblom2006}. The conformal
Gamma-driver with auxiliary $\Upsilon^i$ follows Eqs.~(60)--(62) of
Ref.~\cite{lindblom2008}; the improved $\widehat H_a,\theta_a$ driver follows
Eqs.~(9) and (11), with the combined characteristic system mapped from
Appendix B of Ref.~\cite{lindblom2009}.

\section{Pointwise physical exponent estimator}

At each actual Cartesian cell center, with $X^i=x^i-x^i_{\rm puncture}$,
the implemented production quantity is
\begin{equation}
 q_{\rm loc}=-\frac{1}{6}X^k\gamma^{ij}\partial_k\gamma_{ij}.
\end{equation}
The physical metric and derivative are reconstructed from $\Psi$, $\Phi$, and
the reference two-jets; no additional derivative is taken.  The offline lapse
diagnostic is
\begin{equation}
 p_{\rm loc}=X^k\partial_k\ln\alpha,
 \qquad \alpha=(-g^{00})^{-1/2}.
\end{equation}
The selection and weighting are
\begin{equation}
 2h\le r<8h,\qquad 8h<R_G/2,\qquad
 w_i=(2h/r_i)^3,
\end{equation}
with weighted variance and
$N_{\rm eff}=(\sum_iw_i)^2/\sum_iw_i^2$.  No clipping, rays, radial
interpolation, binning, or puncture extrapolation is used.

For wormhole data,
\begin{equation}
 \psi_W=1+\frac{M}{2r},\qquad
 q_{\rm loc}(r)=-2r\frac{d\ln\psi_W}{dr}
 =\frac{M}{r+M/2}.
\end{equation}
The first-order-state calculation agrees pointwise to at most
$1.6\times10^{-15}$.  For the stationary trumpet it agrees with the
independent profile derivative to at most $7.1\times10^{-13}$, and Minkowski
is exactly zero.

\section{Direct-FD hard-gate failure}

The independent diagnostic reconstructs $\gamma_{ij}$ on neighboring actual
cell centers and applies AthenaK's fourth-order centered operator directly.
With the current support-box exclusion, the state--FD discrepancy grows from
0.236831 to 0.443482 for the wormhole and from 0.0175538 to 0.0200649 for the
trumpet. The strict executable therefore exits nonzero. A current-build replay
also corrected an older table that had retained values from a superseded
``near any coordinate plane'' mask.

This behavior follows from scale invariance.  For
$\gamma_{ij}=r^{-2q}\delta_{ij}$, write a selected cell as $x^i=h\xi^i$.
Any fixed centered stencil obeys
\begin{equation}
 D_h\gamma(h\xi)=h^{-2q-1}D_1\gamma(\xi).
\end{equation}
Because $X^k=h\xi^k$ and
$\gamma^{ij}=h^{2q}\delta^{ij}$, the discrete contraction
$X^k\gamma^{ij}D_k\gamma_{ij}$ is independent of $h$ at fixed $r/h$.
Unless the stencil is exact for the singular power, the bias cannot converge
away.  The weighted pure-power limits for this stencil and shell are about
0.989337 for $q=1$ and 1.575776 for $q=2$.

Thus the claim ``local puncture-exponent estimator established'' is not made
under the controlling same-shell comparison, despite the analytic success of
the first-order-state path.

\section{Standard $\gamma_0/\gamma_2$ checkpoint}

Runtime \texttt{gamma2} defaults to zero. For fixed $\gamma_1=-1$, production
now adds
\begin{equation}
 \delta\Pi_{AB,t}=-\gamma_2\beta^I C_{IAB},\qquad
 \delta\Phi_{IAB,t}=\alpha\gamma_2 C_{IAB}.
\end{equation}
The device gate checks the forward/inverse characteristic map
$u^{1\pm}=\Pi\pm s^I\Phi_I-\gamma_2\Psi$, the standard symmetrizer, and the
frozen reduction and curl subsidiary damping to $3.33\times10^{-16}$.

A six-case periodic runtime matrix through $t=0.2$ covers
$\gamma_2=0,0.5,1$ with KO off and on.  Reduction and curl histories obey
\begin{equation}
 \|C(t)\|_2=\|C_{\gamma_2=0}(t)\|_2e^{-\gamma_2t}
\end{equation}
with maximum absolute growth-factor errors $7.07\times10^{-7}$ and
$2.87\times10^{-6}$.  The $\gamma_2=0$ histories independently measure the
KO contribution.

For $\gamma_0$, a reduction-clean transverse GH-constraint eigenmode is built
from Eq.~(21) of Ref.~\cite{lindblom2006}.  Matrices at
$\gamma_0=0.25,0.5,1$, with KO off and on, obey
\begin{equation}
 \|C_y(t)\|_2=\|C_y(0)\|_2
 e^{-(\gamma_0/2+\lambda_{\rm KO})t}
\end{equation}
to $5.35\times10^{-7}$.  The largest accompanying reduction norm is
$7.19\times10^{-18}$ and curl is zero.  These are local linearized/robust
subsidiary tests, not nonlinear black-hole, long-time, SMR, or GPU
qualification.

\section{Hyperbolic gauge and static-reference regularization}

The evolved state now includes four $\widehat H_A$, four $\theta_A$, and three
$\Upsilon^i$ fields.  Exact frame-motion terms, the advective 1+log target,
the conformal Gamma-driver target, nonzero-$\widehat H$ source terms, combined
characteristics, communication, output, and restart paths are included.

Raw ordinary-gauge frame components are singular at the puncture.  For static
references the stored variables are therefore
\begin{equation}
 \delta\widehat H_A=\widehat H_A-\widehat H^{\rm ref}_A,
 \qquad
 \delta\theta_A=\theta_A-\theta^{\rm ref}_A.
\end{equation}
Physical sources reconstruct the unchanged raw fields.  With this
equation-preserving representation, the exact trumpet is a roundoff fixed point
through $t=1$ at $16^3$, $24^3$, and $32^3$.

Time-dependent-reference subtraction uses a minimal mixed jet containing the
twelve components $\partial_t\partial_i\partial_q$ for each scalar reference
jet. Staged kernels form $\partial_t\partial_iH_A^{\rm ref}$ and analytically
differentiate
\begin{equation}
 \theta_A^{\rm ref}=-\beta^i\partial_iH_A^{\rm ref}
 -(\Omega_{At}{}^B-\beta^i\Omega_{Ai}{}^B)H_B^{\rm ref}.
\end{equation}
The $\delta\theta_A$ equation subtracts the resulting
$\partial_t\theta_A^{\rm ref}$. Closed-form mixed-jet tests pass to
$2.22\times10^{-16}$; independent time-difference oracles pass for smooth
lapse, moving-frame, and interior generic references with maximum scaled
errors $4.94\times10^{-14}$, $2.89\times10^{-15}$, and
$6.71\times10^{-13}$.

The prescribed generic transition is clamped at $t=0$. A centered validator
crossing that $C^2$ endpoint gives the preserved negative result
$2.65\times10^{-6}$. An endpoint-aware fourth-order forward stencil instead
checks the relevant one-sided evolution derivative and passes with scaled
error $1.77\times10^{-17}$, returning to the centered stencil at interior RK
stages. A fixed-grid temporal ladder through $t=0.008M$ gives variable-step
RK4 effective L2/Linf orders 4.003/4.006 for the 50 Einstein fields and
3.987/3.983 for the 11 gauge fields.

For fourth-order Ref-GH the centered derivative radius is two cells, while the
enabled KO operator reaches three.  All evolved-state histories and convergence
results now discard the full radius-three support.  The offline tensor-product
comparison rejects a target if any contributing source cell has a support box
containing the puncture.  A current-build replay changes only the included
diagnostic volume; its field and constraint arrays are elementwise identical to
the pre-correction payload. The KO-off temporal ladder uses the active
radius-two support and rejects 64 puncture-overlap cells, retaining 152 of 216
native cells; no contaminated point enters its reported orders.

For a regular $r^8\exp(-r^2/w^2)$ perturbation, the masked
$24^3/32^3/48^3$ self-orders at $t=1$ are 4.700 for field L2, 4.742 for field
Linf, 3.948 for native-constraint L2, and 3.015 for native-constraint Linf.
All three cases remain finite. Exact-time L2 orders between $t=0.4$ and $t=1$
are resolution improving and approximately, but not uniformly, fourth order.
The outer faces at $2M$ cannot reach the $r<1M$ analysis region before
$t\simeq1.64M$ at the measured maximum characteristic speeds.

\section{Gaussian singular reference and prescribed trajectory}

The new positive isotropic Cholesky factor is
\begin{equation}
 \sigma_q(r,t)=\exp[-q(t)W(r)\ln(r/M)],\qquad
 W(r)=\exp[-(r/R_G)^2].
\end{equation}
A quintic endpoint-flat $q:2\rightarrow1$ trajectory supplies analytic value,
first derivative, and second derivative.  Consequently the reference jets
retain both
\begin{equation}
 \partial_t\ln\sigma_q=-\dot q W\ln(r/M)
\end{equation}
and the $\ddot qW\ln(r/M)$ and
$\dot q^2W^2\ln^2(r/M)$ second-time-derivative structures.

The scan covers $\tau/M=4,8,16$, $R_G/M=2,3,4$, and
$h/M=1/16,1/24,1/32,1/48,1/64,1/128$.  Each dynamic midpoint has a static
$q=1.5$ control.  The staged production cache agrees with the full geometry
oracle to $1.11\times10^{-15}$ at $t=\tau/2$.
