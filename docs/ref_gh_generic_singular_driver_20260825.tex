\begin{abstract}
This report records the first implementation checkpoint for a generic
puncture-adapted reference-frame generalized-harmonic system.  A
reference-independent first-order-state puncture-exponent estimator and a
Gaussian-localized analytic singular reference were implemented and exercised
locally.  The analytic estimator and the prescribed reference-jet scan pass,
but the required direct-finite-difference comparison fails for a scale
invariance reason on the prescribed shell.  Therefore the overall status is
\status{stopped at the initial estimator hard gate}.  No closed-loop control,
hyperbolic gauge-driver, runtime $\gamma_2$, stationary black-hole, SMR, or PVC
qualification claim is made.
\end{abstract}

\section{Primary equations and convention map}

Ordinary GH uses
\begin{equation}
 C^a=\widehat H^a+g^{bc}\Gamma^a{}_{bc},
\end{equation}
while the background-covariant form is
\begin{equation}
 C^a=H_{\rm bg}^a+g^{bc}
       \left(\Gamma^a{}_{bc}-\bar\Gamma^a{}_{bc}\right).
\end{equation}
Equality of the physical constraint gives the sign relation
\begin{equation}
 H_{\rm bg}^a=\widehat H^a+g^{bc}\bar\Gamma^a{}_{bc}.
\end{equation}
The parent source currently specializes to the background wave-map choice,
equivalently $\widehat H^a=-g^{bc}\bar\Gamma^a{}_{bc}$.

The future implementation must translate the complete first-order GH system,
constraint additions, and characteristic fields from Eqs.~(35)--(37) and
(32)--(34) of Ref.~\cite{lindblom2006}; the conformal Gamma-driver with its
auxiliary $\Upsilon^i$ from Eqs.~(60)--(62) of Ref.~\cite{lindblom2008}; and
the improved $\widehat H_a,\theta_a$ driver from Eqs.~(9) and (11), including
the combined characteristic system in Appendix B, of Ref.~\cite{lindblom2009}.
Those changes are not present yet.

\section{Pointwise physical exponent estimator}

At each actual Cartesian cell center, with $X^i=x^i-x^i_{\rm puncture}$,
the implemented production quantity is
\begin{equation}
 q_{\rm loc}=-\frac{1}{6}X^k\gamma^{ij}\partial_k\gamma_{ij}.
\end{equation}
The physical metric and derivative are reconstructed from $\Psi$, $\Phi$, and
the reference two-jets; no additional derivative is taken.  The offline lapse
diagnostic is
\begin{equation}
 p_{\rm loc}=X^k\partial_k\ln\alpha,
 \qquad \alpha=(-g^{00})^{-1/2}.
\end{equation}
The selection and weighting are
\begin{equation}
 2h\le r<8h,\qquad 8h<R_G/2,\qquad
 w_i=(2h/r_i)^3,
\end{equation}
with weighted variance and
$N_{\rm eff}=(\sum_iw_i)^2/\sum_iw_i^2$.  No clipping, rays, radial
interpolation, binning, or puncture extrapolation is used.

For wormhole data,
\begin{equation}
 \psi_W=1+\frac{M}{2r},\qquad
 q_{\rm loc}(r)=-2r\frac{d\ln\psi_W}{dr}
 =\frac{M}{r+M/2}.
\end{equation}
The first-order-state calculation agrees pointwise to at most
$1.6\times10^{-15}$.  For the stationary trumpet it agrees with the
independent profile derivative to at most $7.1\times10^{-13}$, and Minkowski
is exactly zero.

\section{Direct-FD hard-gate failure}

The independent diagnostic reconstructs $\gamma_{ij}$ on neighboring actual
cell centers and applies AthenaK's fourth-order centered operator directly.
The resulting state--FD discrepancy grows from 0.167366 to 0.325534 for the
wormhole and from 0.00869049 to 0.0101752 for the trumpet over the table above.
The strict executable therefore exits nonzero.

This behavior follows from scale invariance.  For
$\gamma_{ij}=r^{-2q}\delta_{ij}$, write a selected cell as $x^i=h\xi^i$.
Any fixed centered stencil obeys
\begin{equation}
 D_h\gamma(h\xi)=h^{-2q-1}D_1\gamma(\xi).
\end{equation}
Because $X^k=h\xi^k$ and
$\gamma^{ij}=h^{2q}\delta^{ij}$, the discrete contraction
$X^k\gamma^{ij}D_k\gamma_{ij}$ is independent of $h$ at fixed $r/h$.
Unless the stencil is exact for the singular power, the bias cannot converge
away.  The weighted pure-power limits for this stencil and shell are about
0.989337 for $q=1$ and 1.575776 for $q=2$.

Thus the claim ``local puncture-exponent estimator established'' is not made
under the controlling same-shell comparison, despite the analytic success of
the first-order-state path.

\section{Gaussian singular reference and prescribed trajectory}

The new positive isotropic Cholesky factor is
\begin{equation}
 \sigma_q(r,t)=\exp[-q(t)W(r)\ln(r/M)],\qquad
 W(r)=\exp[-(r/R_G)^2].
\end{equation}
A quintic endpoint-flat $q:2\rightarrow1$ trajectory supplies analytic value,
first derivative, and second derivative.  Consequently the reference jets
retain both
\begin{equation}
 \partial_t\ln\sigma_q=-\dot q W\ln(r/M)
\end{equation}
and the $\ddot qW\ln(r/M)$ and
$\dot q^2W^2\ln^2(r/M)$ second-time-derivative structures.

The scan covers $\tau/M=4,8,16$, $R_G/M=2,3,4$, and
$h/M=1/16,1/24,1/32,1/48,1/64,1/128$.  Each dynamic midpoint has a static
$q=1.5$ control.  The staged production cache agrees with the full geometry
oracle to $1.11\times10^{-15}$ at $t=\tau/2$.
