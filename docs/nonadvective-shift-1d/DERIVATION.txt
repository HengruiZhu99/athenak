Nonadvective Gamma driver with an adapting reference: formulation and boundary audit

The bulk equations are beta_t=mu*Lambda-eta*(beta-b), b_t=sigma*(beta-b). Neither beta advection nor the old -r source and 1/Omega restoration is present. The independent positive-radius Z4 connection and all native Einstein/lapse/telegraph equations are retained. For eta>0,sigma>0, an exactly stationary state has b=beta and Lambda=0. With eta=sigma=0, the direct control requires Lambda=0 for stationary beta but its stored b is passive. Minkowski beta=b=-r,Lambda=0 is an exact continuum equilibrium; its measured discrete RHS residual is about3.3e-12 at N65.

For the derivative reduction U=(chi_r,g_r,Arr,p/Omega,Theta/Omega,Lambda,alpha_r,beta_r,B_r), write U_t=L U_r+lower-order terms. Coordinate speeds are -eig(L). The exact polynomial is
(z-beta)*[(z-beta)^2-alpha^2 chi/g]^2*[(z-beta)^2-cL^2]*[z^2-beta*z-4mu/(3g)].
The reference b is an additional zero-coordinate-speed variable. The proposed shift speeds therefore follow exactly. A sufficient separation from both advected lapse speeds is 4mu/(3g)>cL^2+abs(beta)*cL. Since cL^2>=alpha^2 chi/g in these retained slicing parameters, this also separates the light speeds. The proposed outer choice makes its margin cL^2+beta^2-abs(beta)*cL, strictly positive for cL>0. Constant mu=4 is blended only over the existing C7 collar r=.8 to.9; positivity of the full blended margin is monitored, not assumed. At normalized scri, mu=3/2 is defective, while mu=9/4 has complete exact scalar eigenspaces. Neither result proves 3D or singular-endpoint well posedness.

Let lambda=(beta+sqrt(beta^2+16mu/(3g)))/2>0, so its physical speed is incoming (-lambda). An exact right eigenvector normalized by delta Lambda=1 has only four nonzero entries:
delta chi_r=-chi*g/2, delta g_r=g^2, delta Lambda=1, delta beta_r=mu/lambda.
It preserves the connection-constraint trace: delta(Lambda-V)=delta Lambda-delta g_r/g^2=0, with boundary metric values fixed. Changes along this vector cannot overwrite an outgoing or tangent characteristic amplitude in the frozen scalar symbol. Exact randomized tests and rational symbolic checks verify the identity.

The implementation evolves three independent endpoint first derivatives chi_r,g_r,beta_r. Their time derivatives are endpoint differences of the corresponding total field rates. On every stage and accepted state, beta is reconstructed as G=-alpha sqrt(chi/g). A scalar projection along the incoming vector enforces mu*Lambda-G_t-eta*(G-b)=0, using the live, penalty-modified geometric rates and the fixed lapse clock. b_t=sigma*(G-b) remains an ODE at the boundary. Lambda is never set independently to zero; the whole incoming jet changes together. Projection is idempotent to floating-point precision in finite perturbed-metric tests. Stored derivative minus finite-difference derivative is reported separately; algebraic compatibility does not establish reduction consistency or numerical stability.

The other boundary conditions remain approximate penalties: the eight native pole numerators S, chi-g, the shear-like trace Arr-g_r/6+2chi_r/3, p,Theta, and Lambda-V. The raw lapse-clock rate is recorded before replacing it with zero. In particular the shear-like trace changes along the incoming vector, by -g^2/6-chi*g/3. It therefore cannot be independently imposed as an extra arbitrary incoming datum along with the driver relation. Retaining it as the old penalty also requires stability testing. Exact nullness, vanishing numerator limits, and a well-posed boundary problem are three distinct statements.
