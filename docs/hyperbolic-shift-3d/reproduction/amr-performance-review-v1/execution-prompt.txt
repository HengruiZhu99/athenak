4. Important findings to carry into implementation

- Sparse stage exchange, boundary reuse and radial field batching are ALREADY enabled. Historical rank-average measured cuts gave no GPU benefit; field batching subsequently removed most of that old imbalance. Do not repeat the old cost model or multiply speedups from separate historical jobs.
- Native Cartesian arrays/ownership are used at regrid, not during RK stages. Evolution uses separate equal contiguous active-cell cuts and independently divided surface rows. Distinguish stage imbalance from regrid bridge cost.
- Default initialization starts with eight native roots and coarsens to eight leaves: it cannot currently run above eight MPI ranks. Native LoadBalance also mishandles skipped/empty-rank metadata; costs [10,1,1] on three ranks expose this in its assignment loops. Do not remove the guard or inject unequal native costs without fixing/testing the contract.
- Full-sized state/base, padded buffers, host geometry and plans are replicated. At N12/15 Cartesian leaves plus36 shell patches, two padded buffers alone cost424MiB/rank. Native fine/coarse state+compensation plus registered send/receive reservations add about1316MiB at capacity104, or405MiB at capacity32, before other structures. Measure actual high-water usage and regrid coexistence.
- Active ordering appends evolved overlap after all nominal block cells. A rank's min/max block span may cover all51 blocks despite selecting only22. DownloadOwnedPadded copies that whole span, once per field, with fresh temporary storage.
- Diagnostic halo Fill increments persisted chi-sign statistics. Different cadence may change counters, running extrema and FastFlow warm starts/seeds, even if evolution fields agree.
- PointKernel::BuildJet consumes fields0–27; 11 fields need second derivatives. Generic radial Prepare currently computes full jets for all31. Preserve the generic API and verify every consumer before masking.
- The coordinated-plan class cannot simply be enabled: chi-floor and measured-partition static assertions, hard-coded ContinuousCoreMesh construction and numerical defaults require adaptation. Reuse transaction/epoch patterns deliberately.
- Native send-buffer sizing merits adversarial verification: each N12 refined-child message carries14^3 values/field while a fine capacity slot has20^3; eight child messages can exceed one slot. This is a credible bound hazard, not an observed overrun. Actual legal topology/destinations determine the payload. Verify metadata and send/receive bounds before any capacity change.
- “Exactly two intervals per rank” is not a general ownership solution. Independent Cartesian and shell assignments can require multiple nominal and overlap segments. Prove any proposed representation and preserve exact owner/tie/overlap semantics.
