Read-only composed constraint-functional attribution
===================================================

The archived private evolution uses LoadComposedMeshJet<3> in RHS, but its
CartesianConformalPatch::Diagnose deliberately retains LoadMeshJet<3>. No
evolution is performed here. The original private build and all its captured
repository/link/source inputs are verified by SHA256 before/after this audit.

A fresh include overlay changes exactly one statement in Diagnose from
LoadMeshJet<3>(q,idx,0,k,j,i) to LoadComposedMeshJet<3>(q,idx,0,k,j,i). Two
standalone executables compile the same check_snapshot.cpp, once against the
unchanged private header and once against the overlay. Both use the original
private radius-four symmetric quadratic ghost reconstruction and analytic
Minkowski reference jets. They read stored binary64 restart states, with no
projection, RHS invocation, time integration, or modification of those states.

The two mesh loaders use identical field values and first/mixed derivatives.
Only diagonal second derivatives change: the original centered fourth-order
Dxx is replaced by the square of centered fourth-order Dx. Both loaders add
the same exact analytic reference jets after differentiating deviations.
Thus this experiment is not a raw composed finite difference of the full
reference-plus-deviation metric.

Let g_ij be the twice-conformal metric, g^ij its inverse, chi the conformal
factor, and b_ij=g_ij/chi the Penrose spatial metric. Write delta for composed
minus original jets; only delta(g_ij,dd) and delta(chi,dd) can contribute to
the changed Hamiltonian. Their change to b's diagonal Hessian is

  delta(b_ij,dd)=delta(g_ij,dd)/chi-g_ij*delta(chi,dd)/chi^2.

All connections and all value/first-derivative curvature terms are identical.
Contracting the linear second-derivative terms of the Ricci scalar gives

  delta R[b] = sum_dij (b^di*b^dj-b^dd*b^ij)*delta(b_ij,dd)
             = chi*sum_dij (g^di*g^dj-g^dd*g^ij)*delta(g_ij,dd)
                 +2*sum_d g^dd*delta(chi,dd).

The evolved physical vacuum Hamiltonian diagnostic therefore changes by

  delta H = Omega^2 * delta R[b].

EvolvedConstraints momentum is constructed from A, first derivatives of A,
metric and chi, the first derivative of P+2Theta, and the fixed Omega gradient.
Its value has no second derivatives. Z is the metric-lowered difference
between Lambda and contracted metric connection and also has no second
derivatives. Theta is stored directly. Hence M, Z and Theta must be identical
pointwise under this loader change. The utility checks these exact equalities,
checks the Ricci difference formula independently at every active cell, and
checks that each actual Diagnose agrees with direct per-cell constraints.

The original Diagnose must first reproduce native HST H/M/Z/Theta at every
selected exact restart time. The N24 checkpoint nearest t=.2 is actually
.20017968749999435. Its preceding output is .17537109374999502. A separate
comparison linearly interpolates diagnostic RMS values between those two
times to .2; it never constructs an interpolated state. N36 has an exact .2
checkpoint. Different native dt/Omega_min and only two resolutions prevent
interpreting the resulting log-ratios as established pure-spatial order.

Consistent composed RHS and composed linear constraint functionals are needed
for the flat constant-coefficient linear closure argument. This attribution
does not establish nonlinear or variable-coefficient discrete product rules,
an energy estimate, global convergence, stability, scri regularity, or BH
admission. The archived momentum/Z/Theta growth is unaffected by the choice
between these two diagnostic functionals.
