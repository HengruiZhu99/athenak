Outcome and scope

The current default eta_inner=0 does not admit a coordinate-stationary future
Schwarzschild trumpet in the regular radial power-law/polyhomogeneous class
specified below. Its alpha^2*chi shift driver becomes subleading, whereas
shift advection has a nonzero leading term. Positive eta_inner=v removes
this leading obstruction. This is a necessary local balance, not existence,
sufficiency, stability, formation, full gauge stationarity, global matching
or dynamic boundary-manifold preservation. No standard moving-puncture
radius or lapse exponent is imposed.

Sign, dimensions and stationary Schwarzschild geometry

Use geometrized units and standard production K_ij=-L_n(gamma_ij)/2.
Lengths r,R,M,S,a have dimension L; alpha,chi,gtilde,beta,c are dimensionless;
K,Lambda,v,eta_inner have dimension 1/L. Let Schwarzschild time be
T=c*t+h(R), c>0, F=1-2M/R, s=sqrt((alpha/c)^2-F). The future BH branch has
outward beta^R=alpha*s and R'(r)>0. The stationary ADM data satisfy

  gamma_RR=c^2/alpha^2,  h'=-c*s/(alpha*F),
  K_R=s_R, K_T=s/R, K=s_R+2s/R.

proof.py verifies g_tt=-c^2 F=-alpha^2+gamma_RR*(beta^R)^2,
g_tR=-cFh'=gamma_RR*beta^R and gamma_RR=F^-1-Fh'^2. Thus the outward
shift and positive K below use the current minus-L_n convention. With
alphaDot=0, beta^R*alpha_R=alpha*(alpha+2)*K, whose first integral is

  [(alpha/c)^2-F]*R^4 = C4*(1+alpha/2)^2.

At a simple lapse-zero cylinder R0, take 0<R0<3M/2, so

  s0=sqrt(2M/R0-1),
  K0=(3M-2R0)/(R0^2*s0)>0,
  alpha_R0=2*(3M-2R0)/[R0*(2M-R0)]=2*K0/s0>0.

The local branch excludes degenerate roots. c is the constant normalization
of the stationary Killing time; c=1 is used in the examples, not proved to
be the eventual normalization of a globally matched layered solution.

Regular radial asymptotics, without assuming bounded derivatives

Assume R-R0=k*r^p plus conormal power/log corrections, k>0,p>0, with the
corresponding differentiated expansions. In particular gtilde radial and
angular Cartesian eigenvalues tend to positive constants, r*d_r gtilde is
bounded, and the second derivatives have the corresponding conormal bounds.
The numerical prototypes use ordinary powers and analytic derivatives.
Bounded metric components alone would not imply this derivative control;
wild oscillatory metrics are outside this conditional argument. Direction-
dependent bounded Cartesian metric limits are allowed: smooth extension
through the puncture point itself is not assumed.

The stationary Schwarzschild formulas imply

  alpha ~ alpha_R0*k*r^p,
  beta^r ~ v*r,  v=alpha_R0*s0/p=2*K0/p>0,
  gamma_rr ~ c^2*p^2/(alpha_R0^2*r^2),
  gamma_tangent ~ R0^2/r^2.

Define zeta=c*p/(alpha_R0*R0). Then

  gtilde_R0=zeta^(4/3), gtilde_T0=zeta^(-2/3),
  chi ~ r^2/[R0^2*zeta^(2/3)],
  Lambda^r = 2*(1/gtilde_T0-1/gtilde_R0)/r + o(1/r).

Thus alpha^2*chi*Lambda=O(r^(2p+1))=o(r). Dividing the actual shift
RHS by r gives betaDot^r/r -> v*(v-eta_inner). At default eta_inner=0
this is v^2>0. A driver cancellation would require Lambda~r^(-1-2p),
which violates the assumed radial derivative expansion. This excludes a
regular stationary endpoint in this class, not transient geometric trumpet
formation, a different singular coordinate chart or a time-dependent limit.

A necessary local repair is eta_inner=v>0, with p=2*K0/eta_inner.
An isotropic chart additionally requires zeta=1, p=alpha_R0*R0/c; neither
isotropy nor its exponent follows from the actual shift. For beta=v*r+
B*r^(1+2p), the first remaining driver term would require
B=-(3/8)*A^2*Cchi*lambda0/[v*(1+2p)] (alpha~A*r^p,chi~Cchi*r^2).
Earlier subleading beta terms must vanish. This illustrates the unsolved
coordinate matching problem; the tested pure R=R0+k*r^p maps only satisfy
the leading damped balance, and their finite-radius damped shift RHS is
nonzero. Eta equality is deliberately not called sufficient.
