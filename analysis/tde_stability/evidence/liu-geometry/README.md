## Provider and numerical regularity

`src/coordinates/kerr_liu.hpp` implements the Liu-Etienne-Shapiro radial map and extrinsic curvature from [arXiv:1001.4077, Eqs.11 and13–15](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1001.4077). The slice has two asymptotically exterior sheets joined at the throat. It is not a horizon-penetrating trumpet. The chosen dimensionless spin is `a/M=.9`, with M=1; throat radius is `.3589724735885168M`, diameter `.7179449471770336M`. Spacing `.0625M` gives11.487 cells across that diameter; `.125M` gives5.744 and does not meet the requested ten-cell criterion.

The metric and K tensors are expressed directly in Cartesian radial/azimuthal vectors. There is no `1/sin(theta)`, angular coordinate, floor, clipping, or excision. The identity `R-r_plus=(r-c)^2/r` removes the throat subtraction/0-over-0 issue. The provider rejects the puncture r=0, nonfinite inputs, invalid mass, and `|a|>=M`. The extremal limit is not claimed.

`Evaluate(mass,a,xyz,gauge,Geometry<T>&)` returns a status and clears the output on entry. `a` is a length; its ratio to mass is the dimensionless spin. The hole is centered at xyz=0 and spins along z. Fields include physical ADM gamma/K/lapse/shift; chi; conformal metric/A; traceK; and conformal Gamma. Cartesian first and second derivatives are generated by compiled forward differentiation through the analytic expressions, without numerical coordinate perturbations. Gamma is `-partial_j(gtilde_inverse^ij)` and includes its first derivative; its type deliberately has no unavailable second derivative. The header accepts the Kokkos inline annotation when included in that environment, but only standalone CPU compilation was tested here.

Gauge choice is mandatory:

- `precollapsed_zero_shift`: smooth positive `alpha=sqrt(chi)`, beta=0; appropriate initial gauge data for a future moving-puncture relaxation or the authorized frozen-reference model.
- `signed_stationary_diagnostic`: analytic rotational shift and the smooth signed stationary lapse. It is negative on the second sheet and zero at the throat. This is for checking continuum equations; it must not bypass the evolution code's positive-lapse validation.

## What the frozen reference changes

For the precollapsed gauge the unsubtracted continuum geometry is nonstationary. At spin.9 on the oblique ray `(x,y,z)/r=(.3,.4,sqrt(.75))`, the metric/K RHS physical norms are:

| r / throat | norm gamma_t | norm K_t |
|---|---:|---:|
| .5 | .0408656 | .0372500 |
| 1 | .1060205 | .0479390 |
| 2 | .1634624 | .0476481 |

In M=1 units these are finite continuum terms, not background truncation errors. Thus `delta_t=F(bg+delta)-F(bg)` has an exact algebraic fixed point at delta=0, as the user proposed, while the corresponding full physical data have nonzero geometric RHS. This fact supports testing the subtraction as a clearly labeled reference-forced model; it does not establish physical vacuum equilibrium or perturbation stability.

The second script quantifies the constraint distinction with a physical, constraint-satisfying mass variation at fixed a/M=.9. The frozen source is held in conformal Z4c variables, not ADM variables. Without projection the change in linear constraint propagation is `-C''[delta_U,F(bg)]`. With algebraic tangent projection P(U), one must differentiate `-C'[U] P(U) F(bg)` and include P'. Both are checked. The source preserves constraints at the reference itself to below1e-11, but nearby mass-family data acquire nonzero momentum and Q sources. At the reference throat, per unit deltaM/M:

| Reference-source treatment | Momentum source physical norm | Q source coordinate norm |
|---|---:|---:|
| Unprojected conformal source | .0756067 | .0279969 |
| Algebraic tangent projection included | .0271821 | .0279969 |

Hamiltonian forcing in this particular axisymmetric direction is symmetry-zero to7.1e-13; no nonzero H source is claimed. Q here is Gamma minus metric-defined Gamma, measured with a coordinate Euclidean norm, not a physical Z norm. Two source-gradient stencils and three mass-variation amplitudes agree on the nonzero responses within2e-7 relative. `reference-forcing-results.json` retains all values and calibration. This is a continuum point audit, not an evolution or an instability prediction.

The future paired controls should therefore distinguish: exact cancellation at the chosen reference; physical full moving-puncture relaxation; and response of constraint-satisfying and constraint-violating perturbations in the modified reference model.
